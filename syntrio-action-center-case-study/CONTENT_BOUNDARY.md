# Syntrio Action Center — Content Boundary

## Public position

> Concept proposal at Mitratech — not implemented.

The original Action Center dashboard was created during the user's Mitratech tenure. The business framing, working user model, responsible-AI principles, design critique, validation plan, and portfolio narrative are retrospective product work.

## Original concept evidence

The public case study may state that the original dashboard contained:

- organisational filters
- summary metrics
- incident trend and category views
- an incident queue
- recommended training and policies
- recent activity
- an internal concept and prototype proposal

## Retrospective material

The following must remain described as retrospective, proposed, or intended:

- business-problem framing
- user-role model and jobs to be done
- incident-to-preventive-action loop
- recommendation anatomy
- rules-first and AI-assisted model
- human-review controls
- privacy, access, and audit principles
- design-review recommendations
- enterprise states
- validation tasks and measures

## Claims that must not appear

Do not state or imply that the concept:

- shipped
- was implemented
- reached production
- used functioning AI
- integrated with PolicyHub or another live product
- was validated through completed user research
- completed formal usability testing
- improved resolution time
- reduced incidents
- increased training completion
- improved audit readiness
- increased adoption, retention, cross-sell, ARR, or revenue
- achieved an accessibility conformance level

## Image boundary

Only the original Action Center dashboard is shown. Different images are scroll positions or crops from that same dashboard.

Related incident, training, policy, permissions, and administration workflows are internal and confidential. They are discussed only at system level and are not recreated as historical product screens.

## Data boundary

- Names and email addresses are synthetic.
- Dashboard values are example interface content.
- Metrics are not design outcomes.
- Case IDs and dates should not be treated as evidence of real operational data.
- Recommendation titles and descriptions are concept examples.

## Role wording

Use until stronger ownership evidence is confirmed:

> Product Designer · concept and prototype contributor

Do not use `sole designer`, `product owner`, `AI product lead`, `lead designer`, or `end-to-end owner` without verification.
